🧠 Unit 1 Review & Grammar Summary
Review sentence patterns before entering the Boss Arena!

1. Asking where someone is from (Self & You)

❓ Where are you from?
👉 I'm from Viet Nam. (I'm from Britain. / I'm from America.)

2. Asking about a third person (He / She / They)

❓ Where's he from? 👉 He's from Singapore.
❓ Where's she from? 👉 She's from Japan.
❓ Where are they from? 👉 They're from Australia.

3. Phonics & Pronunciation Rules

🅰️ The /ə/ sound: America
🅰️ The /iə/ sound: Australia, Malaysia
